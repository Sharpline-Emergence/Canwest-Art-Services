Just Bring Yourself — Watercolour Workshops

with Errol Brimacombe · Chilliwack, B.C.

Three hands-on watercolour sessions for adults of every skill level — from your very first wash to refining landscapes with depth and atmosphere. Small class sizes (6–12 participants) mean plenty of one-on-one guidance.

Paints, brushes, paper & all supplies included — just bring yourself
January 17, 2027 · 1:00–5:00 p.m.

Session 1: Foundations & First Landscape

$125.00 + GST · Chilliwack, B.C.
  • For all skill levels — no experience necessary
  • Watercolour paper, paints, brushes & alternative tools
  • What makes a “good” painting
  • Control & timing in watercolour
  • Wet-on-wet sunsets
  • Guided painting: a far-off mountain landscape with trees & water
Uses Arches watercolour paper & Winsor & Newton Professional watercolours
January 31, 2027 · 1:00–5:00 p.m.

Session 2: Trees, Grounds & Refinement

$125.00 + GST · Chilliwack, B.C.
  • For all skill levels — no experience necessary
  • Elements & principles of a strong painting
  • Refining sunset & mountain technique
  • Detail & intensity across foreground, mid-ground, background
  • Painting trees: living, dead, coniferous, deciduous, distant, fallen, stumps
  • Guided painting: a mountain landscape with the mountain in the mid-ground
Uses Arches watercolour paper & Winsor & Newton Professional watercolours
February 21, 2027 · 1:00–5:00 p.m.

Session 3: Foreground Mountains & Mist

$125.00 + GST · Chilliwack, B.C.
  • In-depth review of the Jan. 17 & Jan. 31 topics
  • Painting a scene with the mountain in the foreground
  • Painting fog & mist rising on a mountainside
  • Dedicated question period
Uses Arches watercolour paper & Winsor & Newton Professional watercolours
