About Us

Welcome to Canwest Art Services. With over 40 years’ experience in the art industry, we offer a wide range of individual and corporate art services.

Our services

  • Fine art and antique appraisals for insurance or estate purposes
  • Free initial consultation by phone, e-mail, or contact us
  • Cleaning and restoration of paintings in all mediums
  • Restoration and conservation of old documents and photographs
  • Corporate fine art and artisanal gifts
  • Art classes in watercolours and oils
  • High quality corporate artwork for commercial spaces

Art supplies including

  • Cranfield Artists’ Oils
  • Gamblin Artists’ Oils
  • Winsor & Newton Watercolours
  • Winsor & Newton Oils
  • Arches Watercolour paper
  • Apollon Gotrick Canvases and cradled birch panels

We look forward to the possibility of serving you.
