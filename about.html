About Us

Welcome to Canwest Art Services, where our extensive experience in the art industry spans several decades. While we have maintained a low public profile, our focus has always been on providing top-notch services to retailers and corporations.

Our range of services includes supplying artwork like photographs and prints for businesses, restaurants, hotel rooms, and lobbies, secure hanging solutions for artwork, and acting as consultants for individuals and corporations seeking art acquisitions.

At Canwest Art Services, we are proud to offer art restoration and conservation services, as well as repairs for antique picture frames. Additionally, we specialize in manufacturing selected bulk quantities of frames for professional artists and sourcing commonly used artists’ materials such as canvases and cradled wood panels.

For the past 50 years, we have been appraising fine art and antiques, operating under various banners associated with the fine art industry. Our corporate gifts division is dedicated to providing gender-neutral, original fine art, and high-end crafts. Whether it’s retirement gifts, commemorating corporate achievements, or seasonal gifts for employees, we offer a wide selection. Our services include boxing, gift wrapping, plaquing (if required), and delivery to your desired destination. From small conference gifts to grand design focal points like large bronzes and sculptures, we cater to all your needs.

Whether you represent a corporation or are an individual art enthusiast, we eagerly await your inquiries. Please don’t hesitate to reach out to us using the “contact us” feature on this website.

Errol Brimacombe & Maureen Kelly
