Shipping & Payment Policy

Orders over $150 (before taxes) ship free to any Canadian province with a post office. Orders over $150 (before taxes) shipping to Whitehorse, Iqaluit, or Yellowknife ship for a flat $30 fee.

Within the BC Lower Mainland, orders of $50 or more, or 3 or more tubes (whichever is less), qualify for free delivery. There’s no minimum order when Cranfield Artists’ Oils accompany an order for Apollon Gotrick canvases or cradled birch panels.

Prepayment by e-transfer or credit card is required before shipping. Once your order is processed, you’ll receive an invoice to review before submitting payment — we’ll never charge a credit card without your prior approval. You’ll be notified when your order ships.
